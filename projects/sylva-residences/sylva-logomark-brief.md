# Sylva Residences — Logomark Design Brief

**22–24 Sylvan Road, Toowong · Phase 1 — Identity (pre-DA)**

This is the sell-now phase: name, wordmark, palette, typography, art direction. Costs little
to hold, and once the DA lands, production moves immediately — the first listing's full pack
(signboard, flyer, Instagram post, Instagram story) is already included in the offer at no
extra charge.

---

## 1. Name — Sylva Residences

Web-search-clear. Not IP Australia–cleared — that check is still outstanding before anything
goes to print or in front of John.

**Why not Sylvan:** "Sylvan Residences" is Pradella's press name for their competing tower at
70 Sylvan Road, a few hundred metres up the same street, plus a second, unrelated 2019 building
at 56–58 Sylvan Road. Same root, same street — a real collision, not a coincidence.

**Why Sylva works:** identical sound, identical treatment originally asked for, nothing
borrowed from a competitor.

---

## 2. Wordmark

**Recommended: Manrope ExtraBold (SYLVA) + Poppins Light, tracked caps (RESIDENCES)**

- Poppins Light is Miyabi's own body face — pairing it in as the companion word quietly signs
  the work as yours, and keeps production simple since it's already in your type stack.
- Matches the original brief: bold modern sans for the name, a restrained sans (not a fancy
  serif) for "Residences."
- Lockup: SYLVA large / RESIDENCES small and tracked, beneath.

**If you want to see it differently before committing** — two solid alternates from the same
research round:

- *Space Grotesk Bold + Marcellus* — more traditional elegance, a quiet tracked serif
  companion. "No romance, all proportion."
- *Libre Franklin Black + Bodoni Moda Light* — shares Understory's own Didone family, so both
  name directions sit on one visual system if they're ever run side by side.

Full set of five pairings, with real Behance references, is in the **Sylva Residences — Brand
Feel** artifact.

## 4. Typography

- **Wordmark:** Manrope ExtraBold (800) / Poppins Light (300), tracked caps.
- **Supporting / body copy:** Poppins Light — consistent with Miyabi's own system.
- **Editorial accent** (optional — signage, long-form pieces): Newsreader, italic. Used across
  both the Understory and Sylva boards as the "editorial register" touch.

---

## 5. Art direction

Full-bleed, photography-led, editorial — not corporate real-estate stock. Dark gradient
overlays for text legibility over warm dusk/interior tones, drawn from the client's own
renders, not generic luxury stock. Numbered, swipeable presentation when shown digitally.

**Compliance note:** every render used in concepting so far is AI-generated. Any use of them
in front of John needs the same AI-disclosure label used everywhere else in this pitch.
