reasoning, this one has just the values.

## Type

| Role | Font | Weight/style | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wordmark — "SYLVA" | Manrope | 800 (ExtraBold) | [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Manrope) |
| Wordmark — "RESIDENCES" | Poppins | 300 (Light), tracked caps | [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Poppins) |
| Editorial accent | Newsreader | 300, italic | [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Newsreader) |

Google Fonts CDN (for any HTML/live use):

```html
<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Manrope:wght@300;800&family=Poppins:wght@300&family=Newsreader:ital,wght@1,300&display=swap" rel="stylesheet">
```

## Alternate wordmark pairings (if Manrope/Poppins isn't the direction you land on)

| Pairing | "SYLVA" | "Residences" |
|---|---|---|
| Bold & carved | Archivo Black | Cormorant, 300 italic |
| Modern & refined | Space Grotesk, 700 | Marcellus |
| Architectural & editorial | Big Shoulders Display, 800 | Newsreader, 300 italic |
| Confident & classic | Libre Franklin, 900 | Bodoni Moda, 300 |

All five (including the recommended Manrope/Poppins pairing) are live side-by-side in the
**Sylva Residences — Brand Feel** artifact for a visual comparison before you commit one to the
logomark.
