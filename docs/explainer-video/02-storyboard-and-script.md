## Look
- Canvas: pure white `#FFFFFF` for open and close, `#1A1A1A` dark band for the UI and fan-out scenes. Ink `#000000`, secondary `#696969`, tint `#F2F2F2`.
- Accent: Miyabi Red `#AC3235`, only on the 雅 mark and the final CTA. Nothing else red, ever.
- Type: Poppins Light (300) for all text. Headlines 2 to 4 words. The MIYABI DESIGN wordmark stays a locked lockup image, never retyped.
- Photography of real work carries the frames. Client brand colours appear only inside the client-brand mockups (scenes 5 to 7), so the red stays meaningful.
- Grade: light bloom, vignette on dark scenes, film grain 0.02 to 0.04. Tilted hero mockups at about 36 to 48 degrees, z-separated for parallax. Hard cuts on motion.
- Music: sparse, minimal, no vocals, ducked under narration. Licensed or generated; confirm rights before publishing.
